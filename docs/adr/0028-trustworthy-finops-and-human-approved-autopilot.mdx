# ADR 0028: Trustworthy FinOps and human-approved Autopilot

FinOps accepts sourced, dated cost evidence and preserves currency. It never synthesizes provider prices,
currency conversion, or savings. Autopilot is a deterministic advisory layer over persisted SLO,
benchmark, qualification, capacity, and cost evidence. Plans are content-digested and human approval is
auditable but side-effect free. Production changes continue through the normal candidate/Release Guard
lifecycle, preserving one owner for deployment mutation.

# v0.7.0 — inference decisions

## Outcome

InferCrane recommends and explains deployment choices from qualified evidence and enforces explicit
SLO, overflow, privacy and cost policy.

## Scope

- recommendation records with provenance, confidence and missing-data disclosure
- model/runtime/GPU/provider/region compatibility and capacity intelligence
- deterministic SLO policy for latency, error, availability and bounded cost signals
- health/queue-driven hybrid overflow to approved external targets
- explanation and audit output for every recommendation and routing change

## Exclusions

No fabricated price/capacity data, autonomous `provider:auto`, semantic routing, silent request
duplication, or model-quality judgment by an LLM.

## Acceptance

Recommendations reproduce from persisted benchmark/capability evidence; insufficient evidence
returns unknown; policy simulations cover oscillation, budget exhaustion, provider recovery,
privacy denial and fail-closed behavior.
